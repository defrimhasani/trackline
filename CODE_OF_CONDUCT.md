# Code of Conduct

Trackline adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Trackline.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub ([@defrimhasani](https://github.com/defrimhasani)). All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including its enforcement guidelines, applies and is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
